A collection of places to find data:

- [Data is Plural](https://www.data-is-plural.com/): A weekly newsletter curated by Jeremy Singer-Vine
- [Tidy Tuesday](https://github.com/rfordatascience/tidytuesday): A weekly data visualization challenge 
- [ICPSR](https://www.icpsr.umich.edu/web): Inter-university Consortium for Political and Social Research based at the University of Michigan. Thousands of datasets from research investigations.
- [Harvard Dataverse](https://dataverse.harvard.edu/)
- [OSF](https://osf.io/): Open Science Framework
- [fivethirtyeight](https://data.fivethirtyeight.com/)
- [MIT Election Lab](https://electionlab.mit.edu/)
- [Pew Research Center](https://www.pewresearch.org/tools-and-resources/)
- [ProPublica](https://www.propublica.org/datastore/datasets)
- [US Government Open Data](https://data.gov/)
- [Awesome Public Datasets](https://github.com/awesomedata/awesome-public-datasets)
- [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets)
